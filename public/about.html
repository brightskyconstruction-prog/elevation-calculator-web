What This Tool Does

The Elevation & Grade Calculator is a free mobile web app designed for everyday use on construction job sites. It replaces paper field books and manual arithmetic for elevation and slope calculations. Add survey points, compare heights between any two points, calculate slope and grade percentage, and retrieve your full history — all from your phone or tablet.

Features

📍

Save Survey Points

Enter and store elevation points by label (PT1, PT2…). Points are saved locally and synced to the cloud when signed in.

📐

Compare Heights

Select any two points to instantly see the elevation difference and which point is higher.

📈

Slope & Grade Calculator

Enter the horizontal distance between two saved points to calculate slope percentage and grade ratio automatically.

🔢

Rod Reading Calculator

Compute derived elevations from benchmark + backsight + foresight rod readings in feet-inches or engineering decimal format.

📋

Retrieve History

Full calculation history is stored per user. Scroll back through past sessions without losing any work.

📶

Works Offline

Install as a PWA from your browser for full offline use — no internet needed on the job site.

Who Uses It

  • Construction foremen — verify subgrade and finish grade elevations for drainage and slabs
  • Pipe layers — calculate invert elevations and check pipe slope for gravity drainage
  • Road crews — confirm subbase and pavement elevation throughout a project
  • Surveyors — quick field check of differential leveling calculations
  • Civil engineering students — practice benchmark and rod reading problems
  • Landscape contractors — grade swales and slopes for proper water runoff

How to Get Started

Open the calculator in any mobile browser and tap Add to Home Screen to install it as an app. No account is required for basic use — create a free account to sync your survey points across devices and access your calculation history from anywhere.
